# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want CleanX to be a practical and respectful open-source project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate clearly, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss tradeoffs with evidence.
- Keep issue and PR comments relevant to the project.
- Respect user privacy and platform boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information.
- Encouraging abuse, spam, credential theft, evasion or platform manipulation.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and may restrict participation from users who violate this code of conduct.
